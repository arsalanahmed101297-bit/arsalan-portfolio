About me

AI that does the work,
not just the talking.

I'm an Agentic AI Engineer focused on turning manual, repetitive business processes into reliable AI systems. My work sits between strategy and execution: understanding how a business actually operates, then designing agents that reason through tasks, use tools, and take action.

From prompt architecture to multi-step agentic workflows, I build systems that are deliberate about context, guardrails, and hand-offs — so the output is useful, not just impressive in a demo. I work with founders, agencies, and growing teams who want practical automation without adding headcount.
